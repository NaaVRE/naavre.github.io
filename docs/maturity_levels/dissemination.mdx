In case you publish research that has been supported by NaaVRE, we would appreciate if you cite the NaaVRE paper or mention NaaVRE in the acknowledgements.

**Cite the NaaVRE paper:** *Zhao, Z., Koulouzis, S., Bianchi, R., Farshidi, S., Shi, Z., Xin, R., Wang, Y., Li, N., Shi, Y., Timmermans, J., Kissling, W.D.: Notebook-as-a-VRE (NaaVRE): From private notebooks to a collaborative cloud virtual research environment. Softw Pract Exp. spe.3098 (2022). https://doi.org/10.1002/spe.3098.*

**Acknowledge NaaVRE:** *"We acknowledge LifeWatch ERIC and the University of Amsterdam for their support of our research conducted through the Virtual Research Environment at https://naavre.net/."*
